**English-market-specific patterns (add these to the watch list):**
1. **Question-stack openers** ("Are you a nomad? Do you miss…?") — reads as engagement bait to this audience; state the tension instead
2. **Conference-brochure vocabulary** ("like-minded individuals", "latest trends", "next level") — nomads have seen a thousand of these; concrete beats polished
3. **Format-announcing hooks** ("Member spotlight!", "a thread 🧵") — lead with the story, not the container
4. **Spotlights without specifics** — no name + no outcome = no social proof; collect the facts first
5. **The Bali comparison is the strongest English-market hook** — both top scorers used it; it names the exact migration this audience is living
