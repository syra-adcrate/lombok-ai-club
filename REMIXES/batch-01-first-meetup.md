### Draft

> Di era digital ini, AI adalah teknologi revolusioner yang akan mengubah masa depan. Ayo bergabung dengan komunitas kami untuk belajar bersama tentang artificial intelligence, machine learning, dan teknologi masa depan lainnya. Acara ini cocok untuk semua kalangan!

### Audit
| Component | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 2/10 | "Kami sangat senang mengumumkan" is announcement-speak — zero payload in the first 8 words, no outcome, no curiosity |
| Body | 3/10 | "Revolusioner", "teknologi masa depan" = hype words the context bans; no what/when/where/cost; abstract, not concrete |
| CTA | 3/10 | Four CTAs (follow, like, share, daftar) split the action; no friction-lowering; 10 hashtags (max is 5) |
| **Total** | **8/30** | ❌ **Rework from hook** |

### Shipped version
> Bikin chatbot pertamamu dalam 60 menit 🤖 — no coding needed.
>
> Meetup pertama AI Club Lombok:
> 📍 [venue]
> 🗓 [date], 4pm
> 💸 Gratis — bawa laptop aja

**Recurring failure patterns to avoid in future remixes:**
1. Announcement-speak openers ("Kami mengumumkan", "We are excited to announce", "PENGUMUMAN") — the #1 hook killer
2. Hype vocabulary ("revolusioner", "epic", "AI revolution") — banned by tone-of-voice rules
3. Multi-CTA stacking (follow + like + share + register) — one action per post
4. Missing logistics (what/when/where/cost) — the body's job is to make showing up easy
5. Formal register in casual channels — WhatsApp is a friend, not an office
