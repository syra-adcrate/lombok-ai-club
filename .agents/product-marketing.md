## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Lombok AI Club — the island's AI & tech community. Weekly meetups, workshops, and build days in Kuta for builders, nomads, and curious locals.
**What it does:** **Lombok AI Club** runs weekly in-person events in Kuta Lombok around practical AI: casual talks and networking, hands-on build sessions, and daytime coworking/build days. It's the gathering point for the island's tech-minded people.
**Product category:** Tech community / meetup group (people search: "AI meetup Lombok", "tech events Kuta Lombok", "digital nomad community Lombok")
**Product type:** Community (in-person events + Instagram + WhatsApp group — both already live)
**Business model:** Free forever — venue partners and sponsors cover costs. Fully independent community brand (no AdCrate affiliation or funnel).

## Target Audience
**Primary — digital nomads & remote workers:** based in or passing through Kuta Lombok (often Bali spillover); developers, indie hackers, designers, content creators, founders. English-first.
**Secondary — tech-curious locals:** freelancers, students, and professionals from Kuta and greater Lombok who want in; bilingual events keep the door open.
**Tertiary — venues & sponsors:** cafes, coworking spaces, hostels, brands wanting access to the nomad/tech crowd.

**Primary use case:** Finding your tech community in Lombok — the one that exists in Bali but was missing here.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Help me find my people — smart builders to talk to, not just party hostels."
- "Help me stay sharp on AI while living somewhere remote and beautiful."
- "Help me meet potential collaborators, cofounders, or clients."

**Use cases (weekly, rotating formats):**
- Casual evening meetups: 1–2 short talks/demos + networking
- Hands-on build sessions: bring a laptop, build something with AI
- Daytime coworking/build days at cafes or coworking spaces

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Lombok has a growing nomad and local tech population but no organized tech community — everything is in Bali.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Bali meetups: a ferry/flight away — not your weekly community
- Online communities: no real-world connection, easy to lurk and drift
- Hostel/party socializing: fun but shallow; no builders in the room
**What it costs them:** isolation, stalled skills, missed collaborations; nomads leave for Bali sooner than they'd like.
**Emotional tension:** "I love it here but I miss having peers" / "AI is moving fast and I'm watching from the sidelines."

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** None — no other AI/tech community in Lombok **[verify periodically]**.
**Secondary:** Bali AI & tech meetups (Canggu/Ubud) — vibrant but not local; you can't build weekly community across the strait.
**Indirect:** Doing nothing / online-only communities / waiting until "back in Bali".

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- The only AI/tech community on the island — first-mover claim on "Lombok tech scene"
- Weekly cadence — short-stay nomads still catch an event
- Practical over theoretical: every event you see or build something real
- Free forever — no funnel, no upsell, community-first
- "Learn AI, then surf" — Kuta setting attracts speakers and visiting experts
**How we do it differently:** in-person, weekly, free, builder-focused, bilingual-friendly.
**Why that's better:** community compounds with frequency; free + no-funnel builds trust fast in a scene allergic to being sold to.
**Why members choose us:** it's the only room on the island where this conversation is happening.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Is this a sales funnel / crypto-bro thing?" | Free forever, independent, no product behind it. Come once and see. |
| "I'm only here 2 weeks" | Weekly events — you'll catch at least one or two. WhatsApp keeps you connected after. |
| "I'm not technical enough" | Formats rotate: talks and coworking days are zero-barrier; build sessions are beginner-welcoming. |

**Anti-persona:** hype-chasers wanting get-rich-with-AI content; people looking for a certified course.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** shallow socializing, working alone, watching the AI wave from the sidelines, Bali FOMO.
**Pull:** real peers, weekly rhythm, free, walkable, demos worth showing up for.
**Habit:** "I'll just stay in my online communities"; solo cafe routine.
**Anxiety:** "Will anyone good actually be there?" — countered by recaps, member spotlights, and speaker quality.
