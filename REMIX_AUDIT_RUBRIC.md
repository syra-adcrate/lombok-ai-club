# Remix Audit Rubric — Lombok AI Club

> How every remix gets audited. Each post is scored on Hook / Body / CTA (1–10 each, 30 max), then adapted to its best-fit platform. Grounded in [.agents/product-marketing.md](.agents/product-marketing.md) — the canonical marketing context (v2: nomads-first, English-first, Kuta home base, weekly events).

---

## Scoring

### Hook (1–10) — does it stop the scroll?
| Points | Criteria |
|---|---|
| +3 | **Specific, not generic** — names a concrete outcome, tool, number, or moment ("build a chatbot in 60 min" not "learn about AI") |
| +2 | **Audience-matched** — instantly clear who it's for (local student vs nomad vs sponsor) and in the right language for them |
| +2 | **First 5–8 words carry the payload** — works even when truncated by the platform |
| +2 | **Curiosity or tension** — a gap, contrast, or question that demands the next line |
| +1 | **Tone** — island-casual, zero hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing" = automatic deduction) |

### Body (1–10) — does it deliver on the hook?
| Points | Criteria |
|---|---|
| +3 | **Concrete value** — real tools, real demos, real logistics (what/when/where/cost); no abstraction |
| +2 | **Right length for platform** — tight for IG/TikTok, roomier allowed on LinkedIn |
| +2 | **One idea per post** — no pillar-mixing (promo OR tip OR recap, not all three) |
| +2 | **Bilingual handled well** — ID/EN mix feels natural, not machine-translated twice |
| +1 | **Scannable** — line breaks, emoji as signposts (sparingly), no walls of text |

### CTA (1–10) — does it convert attention to action?
| Points | Criteria |
|---|---|
| +3 | **One single action** — RSVP, join WhatsApp, comment, share. Two CTAs = split score |
| +3 | **Friction stated and low** — "Free — bawa laptop aja" beats "register now" |
| +2 | **Correct destination for funnel stage** — discovery → Luma link/link in bio; attended → WhatsApp group |
| +2 | **Urgency or scarcity that's true** — date, limited seats; never fake |

**Verdict bands:** 25–30 ✅ Ship it · 18–24 🔧 Fix flagged lines, then ship · <18 ❌ Rework from hook

## Platform adaptation rules

| Platform | Format | Language | CTA target | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** (primary) | Caption ≤150 words, hook in line 1 (before "...more"), 3–5 hashtags max | ID/EN mix | Link in bio → Luma | Reels > static; caption assumes visual exists |
| **WhatsApp** (retention) | Short broadcast message, personal tone, no hashtags | Match group's dominant language | Direct Luma link | Reads like a friend's message, not a broadcast |
| **TikTok** | Hook must be spoken/on-screen in first 2s; caption is secondary | ID-first | Comment or bio link | Repurposed from IG Reels |
| **LinkedIn** | Longer OK (100–200 words), first 2 lines pre-fold, no emoji spam | English | Luma link in comments or post | Credibility/sponsor angle; recap + partnership content |
| **X/Twitter** | ≤280 chars or short thread | English | Link | Only if content is nomad/builder-angled |
